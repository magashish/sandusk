Ottawa County Clerk of Courts

Website Accessibility Improvements Report

Standard: WCAG 2.1 Level AA  |  Date: May 2025  |  Prepared by: Digital Kingdom Interprises

Project Scope: Audit and remediation of accessibility barriers across the Ottawa County Clerk of Courts website (ottawacountyclerkofcourts.com) to meet ADA compliance requirements under WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Changes cover semantic HTML structure, ARIA attributes, keyboard navigation support, screen reader compatibility, heading hierarchy, image alt text, form labeling, and link context.
